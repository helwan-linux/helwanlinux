Helwan Mines Pro

Overview

Helwan Mines Pro is a professional reimagining of the classic Minesweeper for Helwan Linux.

It is designed as a high-stakes test of memory, speed, and mental sharpness.

What Makes This Version Unique?

  • ⚡ Data Decay Mechanic: Revealed numbers are not permanent. They fade away after 5 seconds, turning into dots. You must memorize the board to survive.
  • ⏲️ Time Pressure: You have a strict 60-second countdown to clear the entire field before the big explosion.
  • 🎨 Helwan Native Design: A clean, Nordic-inspired UI designed to integrate with the Helwan Linux ecosystem.
  • 🧠 Mental Workout: The game turns simple logic into a dynamic brain sport by forcing you to manage shifting information.

How to Play

  • The Objective: Reveal all safe cells and avoid clicking on the hidden mines (☢️).
  • Memorize Quickly: Once a number appears, memorize it immediately. After 5 seconds, it disappears, leaving you with only your instincts.
  • Use the Interface: Access the Game and Help menus for manual instructions, restarting, or exiting the application.
