Helwan Snake - Ultimate Edition

Overview

Helwan Snake is an arcade snake game for Helwan Linux. Guide the snake, eat apples, collect the golden apple for bonus points, and avoid walls and collisions with the snake's own body.

Gameplay

  • Regular Apples: Eating a regular apple grows the snake and adds 1 point.
  • Increasing Speed: The game speed increases by 2 FPS every 5 points.
  • Golden Apple: A golden apple may appear randomly during play. It flashes while active and disappears after 70 moves.
  • Golden Apple Bonus: Collecting it grows the snake by two segments and awards 3 points. If the speed is above 12 FPS, it is reduced by 1 FPS.
  • Collisions: Hitting the screen boundaries or the snake's own body ends the game.

Game Menus

  • Main Menu: Play Game, About, and Exit.
  • About: Displays the game information and a Back button.
  • Pause Menu: Resume the game or return to the Main Menu.
  • Game Over: Displays the final score and offers Play Again or Main Menu.
