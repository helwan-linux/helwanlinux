Helwan 2048 Special Edition

Overview

An advanced, custom-engineered version of the classic 2048 game, uniquely tailored and optimized exclusively for Helwan Linux.

Special Features

Unlike standard 2048 clones, this edition introduces strategic special tiles that change the gameplay dynamics:

  • 🔴 BOMB Tile (BOMB): Merging or triggering a bomb tile clears the entire row/column and rewards bonus points.
  • 🟣 JOKER Tile (JOKER): Acts as a wildcard that instantly matches and doubles any adjacent number.
  • 🟡 VIRUS Tile (VIRUS): A ticking danger! If left unmerged, it consumes adjacent standard tiles with each move.
  • ⚫ BLACK HOLE Tile (HOLE): A rare strategic tile that consumes adjacent low-value number tiles, creating space on the board while rewarding the player.
  • 🔵 MIRROR Tile (MIRROR): An interactive tile that reflects an adjacent number, allowing players to manipulate the board and create new merging opportunities.
  • ↩️ Undo Move System: Allows players to step back their last move and recover from critical mistakes.
  • 🏆 High Score Persistence: Automatically saves the player's best score locally.
  • 🎨 Modern UI & Sidebar: Includes Quick Restart, Undo, score tracking, high-score display, and an integrated About dialog.
  • 🎯 Strategic Gameplay: Special tiles introduce risk, reward, board manipulation, and tactical decision-making beyond traditional 2048 mechanics.
